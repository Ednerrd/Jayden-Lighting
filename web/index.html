What you're looking at: the virtual rig has 8 RGBW pars on the truss, 4 moving heads, 2 strobes and 6 floor uplights. Every light value here came out of the same code that drives real DMX. The two test tracks are synthesized (and analyzed here in the browser, like yours), so their "real structure" lane is known exactly. Your own tracks get analyzed on this device with the same code, ported from Python. Pre-analyzed reads the whole song first and blacks out the beat before each drop. Live hears it as it plays, so its colors in the bottom lane show when it figured each section out.
